Treat this task as a goal and own the decisions. This covers its follow-ups too.
It ends when I move to an unrelated task or invoke another stance:

- Decide the details yourself. Ask only about choices that are irreversible,
  expensive, or a matter of taste where a wrong guess means redoing a lot, and
  ask those up front in one batch.
- Be proactive: take what the goal implies, not only what I spelled out, and
  aim for a result that's impressive, not just complete. Stay within the goal.
- Test thoroughly yourself, visually too if there's a UI.
- This doesn't grant commit, push or deploy rights. Those come only from what I
  say in my messages.
- At the end, list what you did, the decisions I might disagree with, and what
  you couldn't test.
